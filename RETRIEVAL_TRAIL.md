# RETRIEVAL TRAIL — what the record shows about located or obtained material

*Built 2026-08-06. Method: a 4-category lexicon sweep (materiel, biological,
custody/program language, contractor/lab names) produced 1,609 hits across 60
documents; every hit was then LLM-evaluated in context (claim, claimant, custody
chain, the document's own disposition) → 56 non-noise leads, 29 strong. Full
per-document evaluations in `records/leads_per_doc/`. Passages are VLM
transcriptions — verify against source pages before quoting. Paragraphs marked
[annotation] add context from outside the corpus and are labeled as such.*

**The question:** is there a hint in the government's own released paper that it —
or a contractor — ever located or obtained a craft, debris, or biological
specimens? **The short answer:** the record documents, at minimum, that the
*machinery* for recovering and analyzing claimed UAP material existed and ran
repeatedly from 1947 on; that the FBI Director personally fought for access to
"discs recovered" by the Army; that the bodies/crash-retrieval narrative has
lived inside government files since 1949-50 and reached sworn congressional
testimony in 2023-24; and that every specific custody chain the record lets us
follow ends mundane, hoax, or unresolved — never in confirmed exotic material.
Both the claims and the government's denials are in this corpus, with page
numbers.

---

## 1. The custody machinery was real — and we can watch it run

The releases document a standing, formalized pipeline for physical UAP evidence:

- **Standing orders**: "Any physical evidence of the sighting will be forwarded by
  most expeditious means to Commanding General, Air Materiel Command"
  (FBI 62-83894 Section 6, p.12). A permanent channel to Wright Field.
- **It ran**: a disc "TRANSPORTED TO WRIGHT FIELD BY SPECIAL PLANE FOR
  EXAMINATION" (Section 1, p.70) · agents "DISPATCHED TO SCENE AND RECOVERED
  DISK TAKEN ONI OFFICES AND EXAMINED" by Navy bomb and electronics experts
  (Section 1, p.99) · "specimens of an alleged Flying Saucer" analyzed by the
  **T-2 Analysis Division at Wright Field** (Section 3, p.164) · a soil sample
  from a claimed saucer depression given a petrographic-geologic exam by the
  **FBI Laboratory**, then returned to Air Materiel Command, Wright-Patterson
  (Section 4, p.56) · charred material from the **Socorro/Zamora site collected
  and retained by the Albuquerque FBI office** (Section 9, p.235).
- **Every ending we can see**: the T-2 specimens were plaster of paris with
  electrical resistors — hoax. The Maury Island fragments — slag, judged a hoax
  after two AAF intelligence officers died flying samples home (Sections 2-3).
  The rancher's find — a Japanese incendiary balloon remnant (Section 7, p.80).
  The Georgia "saucer occupant" — a shaved rhesus-family monkey, per an anatomy
  professor (Section 7, p.160). The Milwaukee metallic object (13"×8"×5",
  police-recovered, 1957-era, Section 10, p.172) and the Socorro charred sample —
  **inconclusive in the file**, no recorded resolution.

**What this proves:** the government took physical claims seriously enough to
build and exercise recovery-and-analysis infrastructure. **What it does not
prove:** that the pipeline ever ingested anything non-mundane — in the released
record, it demonstrably never did.

## 2. The friction — Hoover's own handwriting

The single most striking custody artifact in Release 1 (Section 1, pp.127-131):
asked to cooperate with Army Air Corps Intelligence in July 1947, **J. Edgar
Hoover wrote in his own hand**:

> "I would do it but before agreeing to it we must insist upon full access to
> discs recovered. For instance in the [illegible] case the Army grabbed it and
> would not let us have it for cursory examination."

The file then does something the UFO literature has argued about for decades: a
typed follow-up memo **quotes the illegible word as "the Ia. case"**, and records
General Schulgen assuring Hoover of "complete cooperation" and instructions "to
make recovered discs available to the FBI." [annotation: the object in that
mid-July 1947 case is generally identified with a hoax disc; the historical
debate read the scrawl as "La." (a Shreveport, LA shop-made gag disc) — this
file's own transcription says "Ia."] Either way, the sentence documents two
things nobody disputes: **the Army was physically seizing reported discs in July
1947, and the FBI Director believed access to them was worth demanding.**

## 3. The bodies claims — the narrative's paper roots, 1949 → sworn testimony 2024

The crash-and-bodies story is *in* the government's files — as recorded claims:

- **The Hottel memo, verified in this corpus** (Section 5, p.68): Guy Hottel,
  SAC Washington, to Director FBI, **March 22, 1950** — "An investigator for the
  Air Forces stated that three so-called flying saucers had been recovered in
  New Mexico… each one was occupied by three bodies of human shape but only 3
  feet tall, dressed in metallic cloth of a very fine texture." Filed, no
  investigation recorded. [annotation: the most-viewed FBI Vault document ever;
  its informant chain is traced by historians to the Silas Newton/Leo GeBauer
  "Aztec" fraud, which the FBI itself later prosecuted for an unrelated con.]
- Same section, p.44: an anonymous source describes **two recovered craft — one
  with charred bodies, one with occupants "in a perfect state"** — routed to a
  "US Research Bureau, Los Angeles." Unresolved in file. p.77: the Koehler claim —
  a disc "found intact, with eighteen three-foot tall human-like occupants, all
  dead" (Mojave). Unresolved in file. [annotation: Koehler is a known node of
  the same 1949-50 rumor wave.]
- **The French official study in this release** (255_413270, "UFOs and Defense")
  carries Colonel Corso's cadaver-in-glass claim (p.48) and the Marcel/DuBose
  Roswell affidavits including the debris-substitution allegation (p.72) —
  presented there as claims, not findings.
- **Sworn, modern, in this corpus**: Grusch (26 Jul 2023, pp.16, 31, 44) — a
  "multi-decade UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering program,"
  "nonhuman spacecraft," and "biologics… nonhuman" — and Elizondo (13 Nov 2024,
  pp.22, 43) — retrieval programs "Yes," possession of bodies "Yes. Yes, sir."
  Asserted under oath; no substantiating documents accompany them in the record.

## 4. The denial ledger — equally on the record

- **1947, internal, candid** (18_100754, 1946-47 AMC file, p.10): "the lack of
  physical evidence in the shape of crash recovered exhibits which would
  undeniably prove the existence" of the discs. [annotation: this is the
  language family of the Twining/AMC opinion — the government's own
  secret-at-the-time statement that, as of late 1947, it held no crash exhibits.
  For the 1947 era it is the strongest counter-evidence in the corpus.]
- **1952-era, public posture** (Sub A, p.37): "we have no authentic physical
  evidence that they are interplanetary" — and an Air Force official denying
  possession of "any parts of flying saucers" (p.68).
- **Hoover, directly** (Section 9, p.281): "the FBI has never had custody of an
  occupant from a foreign planet." A denial that exists because citizens kept
  asking the question.
- **2026** (AARO FY25, p.10): "To date, no evidence suggests that a USG or
  private entity has ever captured or exploited UAP-derived materials." AARO's
  Historical Record Report (in corpus) reaches the same conclusion for the
  named historical programs, while documenting **KONA BLUE** — the proposed DHS
  program to *receive and reverse-engineer* UAP materiel, drafted on the
  premise such materiel might exist, and never approved.

## 5. Verdict, honestly stated

- **Supported by the released record:** a real recovery-and-analysis machine
  (1947-64) with named facilities (Wright Field T-2, FBI Lab, ONI, AMC/MCIS);
  real inter-agency seizure friction at the top (Hoover's hand); the bodies
  narrative circulating *inside* official files since 1949-50; modern sworn
  testimony asserting retrieval programs; and a 2019-era program proposal
  (KONA BLUE) premised on the possibility of materiel.
- **Not supported:** any followable custody chain that ends in confirmed
  non-mundane material or biology. Every chain the record lets us trace
  terminates in hoax, balloon, monkey, plaster — or in silence.
- **The honest tension to keep:** the same corpus contains sworn "yes, bodies"
  testimony (2023-24) and flat "no evidence" findings (1947 AMC → 2026 AARO).
  The record, as released, holds both without resolving them.
