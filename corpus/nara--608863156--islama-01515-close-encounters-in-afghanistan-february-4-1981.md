SUBJECT: CLOSE ENCOUNTERS IN AFGHANISTAN

1. (C) ENTIRE TEXT.

2. I AM CONVINCED THAT THERE IS SUBSTANCE IN THE MANY
TRAVELLERS' REPORTS FROM AFGHANISTAN THAT THE RED ARMY IS
USING A TYPE OF VERY POWERFUL, HIGH INTENSITY LIGHT THERE.
THE LIGHT MAY BE USED IN CONNECTION WITH PERIMETER DEFENSE
AND/OR ROAD SECURITY.
