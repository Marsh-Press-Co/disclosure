# NASA-UAP-D030, STS-80 Unidentified Object Image 1, 1996

## Page 1

*Image: A color photograph taken from a spacecraft window. The left side of the frame shows a view of Earth, appearing as a bright, light-blue surface with white cloud cover. The right side of the frame shows the interior structure of the spacecraft and reflections on the window glass. In the center, against the dark background of space, is a small, bright, elongated white object. On the left edge of the film frame, there are red alphanumeric characters oriented vertically: "67", a square symbol, and "18". A partial red marking is visible on the right edge.*
