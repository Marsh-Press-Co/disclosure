JAL UFO INCIDENT: Summary White Paper, by Philip J. Klass 
In early January, 1987, extensive news media coverage was 
given to a UFO incident involving a Japan Air Lines 747 crew that 
had occurred on the evening of Nov. 17, 1986, while flying 
over Alaska, enroute from France to Anchorage. Initial media 
accounts, understandably, were based largely on the six-week old 
recollections of the JAL pilot, Capt. Kenju Terauchi. 
In early March, the Alaskan Region of the Federal Aviation
