# FBI-UAP-D031, Digital Rendering, “Large Triangle with Red Lights,” 2023

## Page 1

*Image: A nighttime photograph of a dark, triangular craft hovering in a clear, star-filled sky. The craft features three glowing red lights, one positioned at each of its vertices.
