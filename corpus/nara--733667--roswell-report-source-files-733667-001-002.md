On November 17, 1986 Japan Airlines Flight 1628, an all cargo Boeing 747, was 
en route from Kef lavik, Iceland to Anchorage, Alaska. At 0219 UTC (November 18, 
1986) the captain of Flight 1628 reported traffic at his altitude 1 mile in front 
of his aircraft. The unidentified traffic was later reported visible on JAL 
1628's weather radar and intermittently by Anchorage Center and the Military 
Regional Operations Center. JAL 1628 reported that the traffic stay ed in his 
vidn ity during a descent and turns, including a 360 degree turn. At 0253 UTC 
UJ JAL 1628 reported that the unidentified traffic was no longer in sight.
