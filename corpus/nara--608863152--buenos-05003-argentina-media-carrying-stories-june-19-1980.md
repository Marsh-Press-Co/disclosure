ARGENTINA MEDIA CARRYING STORIES SIMILAR TO ONE QUOTED  
BELOW, WHICH SAYS NASA ENGINEER OF ARGENTINE ORIGIN  
SENT TO HIS MOTHER A TAPE SAYING THAT A NASA EXPERIMENT  
WOULD CREATE FLYING SAUCER SHAPES IN THE ARGENTINE  
SKY. RECENTLY, THERE HAVE BEEN MANY REPORTS OF UFO  
SIGHTINGS HERE.  
REQUEST THAT NASA RESPOND TO THIS STORY  
TO WASHINGTON MEDIA, SO THAT THE RESPONSE CAN  
BE TRANSMITTED BY NEWS AGENCIES TO SOUTHERN CONE  
AREA, WHERE THE STORY CITED BELOW WILL GENERATE  
MUCH INTEREST.  
THE FOLLOWING STORY APPEARED IN THE BUENOS  
AIRES HERALD OF JUNE 19, FROM NOTICIAS ARGENTINAS,  
ON ARGENTINE VERSION OF THE ASSOCIATED PRESS:

"CORDOBA: THE UFOS THAT WERE SEEN OVER THE COUNTRY
LAST SATURDAY MAY HAVE BEEN THE RESULT OF AN EXPERIMENT
BEING CARRIED AT BY THE NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE
ADMINISTRATION (NASA) OF THE US, CALLED
"FIREWHEEL."
UNCLASSIFIED

UNCLASSIFIED

PAGE 02    BUENOS 05003 191724Z

"LOCAL TELEVISION CHANNEL 12 REVEALED YESTERDAY,
THAT IN A TAPE SENT TO HIS MOTHER, MARIO HECTOR ACUNA,
AN ARGENTINE ENGINEER WORKING FOR NASA, GAVE THE
FOLLOWING INFORMATION: "ON MAY 23 WE WILL LAUNCH A
SATELLITE, LOOK UP AT THE SKY FROM THE JUNE 8 TO 15,
ABOUT MIDNIGHT. YOU WILL PROBABLY ONLY SEE A FLYING
SAUCER," SAYS ACUNA IN THE TAPE, "BUT IT WILL HAVE BEEN
CREATED BY US. THE PROJECT IS CALLED 'FIREWHEEL', AND
IT CONSISTS OF INJECTING AN ARTIFICIAL CLOUD WHICH
WILL CREATE SOMETHING LIKE A COMET, AND THAT IS WHAT
YOU ARE GOING TO SEE."
