FOR OCI

SUBJ: TUNISIAN FIREBALL

REF: TUNIS 3222

1. PHENOMENA ALMOST IDENTICAL THAT OF REFTEL SEEN BY MANY
OBSERVERS TUNIS EVENING JULY 13 INCLUDING EMBASSY OFFICIALS.

2. AGAIN CONSISTED OF TWO PARTS ONE GREENISH-BLUE CIRCULAR
CLOUD ABOUT SIZE OF FULL MOON APPEARING A FEW DEGREES TO WEST
OF POLAR STAR AND WELL BELOW IT WITH RESPECT TO HORIZON. OTHER
PHENOMENON A FEW DEGREES TO RIGHT OF POLAR STAR AND HIGHER
ABOVE HORIZON THAN FIRST PHENOMENON. SECOND APPEARED AS STAR
WHICH THEN EXPLODED FORMING GREENISH CIRCULAR CLOUD WHICH
QUICKLY BECAME MISSHAPEN AND SPREAD OUT ACROSS SKY TO DIS-
APPEAR. SOME REPORTED NOTHING IN CLOUDY PHASE OF SECOND PHENOMENA
SHORT-LIVED BRIGHT LIGHTS.

3. WOULD APPRECIATE KNOWING EXPLANATION WHICH SMITHSONIAN MAY
HAVE FOUND FOR THESE PHENOMENA.

JCS-IL
