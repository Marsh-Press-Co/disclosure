SUBJECT: REQUEST FOR INFO ON POSSIBLE SATELLITE BREAK-UP
- OR OTHER AERIAL PHENOMENA

1. OFFICIAL OF ASECNA, REGIONAL AFRICAN AIR TRAFFIC
SAFETY ORGANIZATION, CONTACTED EMBOFF ON JULY 30 WITH
QUERY ON AERIAL PHENOMENON. ACCORDING TO OFFICIAL,
DURING NIGHT OF JULY 27, SEVERAL CALLERS REPORTED TO
LOME AIRPORT THAT THEY SAW A BURNING OBJECT FLYING LOW
IN THE SKY ON NORTH-SOUTH TRAJECTORY. OFFICIAL ASKED
IF WE HAD ANY INFO ON ANY POSSIBLE SATELLITE BREAK-UP
OR SIMILAR PHENOMENA.

2. PLEASE ADVISE IF YOU HAVE ANY RELEVANT INFO ON THIS
PHENOMENON WHICH WE COULD PROVIDE TO TOGOLESE. KORN
