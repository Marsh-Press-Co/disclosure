ABOUT AARO
UAP
UAP are sources of anomalous 
detections in one or more domains (i.e., 
airborne, seaborne, spaceborne, and/or 
transmedium) that are not yet attributable 
to known actors and that demonstrate 
behaviors that are not readily understood 
by sensors or observers.  
“Anomalous detections” include, but 
are not limited to, phenomena that 
demonstrate apparent capabilities  
or material that exceed known state-of-
the-art capabilities.
